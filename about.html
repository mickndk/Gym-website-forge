Our story

Coaching first.
Everything else second.

Forge opened in 2019 with one rule: every session has a coach watching, correcting and programming — not just a room full of equipment.

Built by coaches, not franchise owners

We started Forge because Johannesburg had plenty of big-box gyms and not enough places where a coach actually knew your name and your squat number. Five years on, we're still capped at small class sizes on purpose — growth was never the goal, good training was.

Every member goes through an onboarding assessment before their first class, so coaches know your history, injuries and goals from day one.

What we stand for

The four things every coach is trained to prioritise in every session.

01

Form before load

Weight goes up once technique is solid — never before.

02
